# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Shengtai Yao, Eitan Levin, and Mateo Díaz for developing a clear framework for continuity, symmetry, and universality when input dimensions grow, and for making the paper’s assumptions concrete enough for independent certificates and direct stress tests.

This repository is a reproduction and documentation companion. It preserves the citation and historical evaluator records, makes premise and activation limits visible, and does not claim author endorsement or transfer scientific authorship to the maintainer.
