# Claim 5 source audit

The relevant paper anchors are Equations 9–10, Theorems 4.9–4.10, and
Appendix C.2–C.3. The domain is the compact quotient of measurable symmetric
`[0,1]`-valued graphons under zero cut distance.

The continuity premise is traced to Borgs, Chayes, Lovász, Sós, and
Vesztergombi, *Convergent Sequences of Dense Graphs I* (2008), Theorem 2.7,
arXiv:math/0702004. The density premise is traced to Diao, Guillot, Khare, and
Rajaratnam, *Differential Calculus on Graphon Space* (2015), Theorem 2.2,
arXiv:1403.3736. The latter explicitly proves by Stone-Weierstrass that the
linear span of simple homomorphism densities is uniformly dense.

The paper's constructive step has exact quantifier `every finite simple graph
F`. For `m=|V(F)|`, Equation 9 uses `(a_e,b_e)=(1,0)` on edges of `F` and
`(0,1)` on non-edges. Isolated vertices integrate to one, so they do not alter
the density.
