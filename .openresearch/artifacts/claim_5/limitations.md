# Claim 5 limitations and deviations

- Compactness, cut-continuity of simple homomorphism densities, and density of
  their span are primary-source premises, not proof-assistant formalizations.
- The finite graph sweep is exhaustive only through six labeled vertices; the
  universal scope is handled by the symbolic bit-constraint derivation.
- Exact step graphons are validation instances, not a replacement for the
  graphon-space density theorem.
- The Appendix C.3 tensor implementation is an existence construction. No
  efficiency or fixed-order claim is made; the paper itself allows order,
  width, and depth to grow with `m`.
- No GPU is used; numerical libraries are restricted to one thread.
